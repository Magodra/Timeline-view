Class templates

timeline.templates holds functions that return extra CSS classes. Here they colour tasks by a priority attribute in the data, strike out finished ones, shade weekends and every other bar line, and outline a frozen timeline. The lighter band and the flag inside the bars are drawn by CSS (::before and ::after) on those classes. The round initials are real elements, added with a task layer.

The task_text template puts a real button in the "Payment API" bar: not clicked yet.

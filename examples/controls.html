Built-in controls

With the controls option the view brings its own editing controls, so a page needs no buttons of its own:

  • The menu above the heading adds a timeline ("+ Timeline"), named "New timeline" and ready to rename.
  • Hover over a task, milestone or label (or select it) and press × to take it off that timeline.
  • On each name label, ↑ and ↓ move the timeline; click the name to rename it, then press Enter or click elsewhere to save, or Escape to cancel.

Every control calls the same methods a host would (addTimeline, removeEntry, moveTimelineUp, renameTimeline, ...), so it fires the usual events, shown below, and with a REST backend it is saved. Dragging works as always.

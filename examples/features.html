Features

Several timelines, a month scale, month and year durations, milestone symbols, names on the right, and callout labels at different heights above and below the bar. "Support" runs past the timeline's finish, so its bar ends in an arrow. "Support", "Launch" and "Security audit" have their own color. The red "Today" line is a global marker: without absolute each timeline has its own time axis, so it is drawn on each timeline separately. Click a task or a timeline to select it, Ctrl+Click (Cmd+Click on a Mac) to select several, and click a selected item again to deselect. The buttons below are outside the component and move the selected timelines with moveTimelineUp / moveTimelineDown, or remove the selected tasks or timelines with removeTask / removeTimeline. "Rename" renames the selected timeline with renameTimeline, or the view with renameView when no timeline is selected. "Add timeline" adds an empty timeline with the name you type, spanning all the others, with addTimeline.

Absolute timelines

A second view with absolute: true. Each timeline has its own start and finish dates, and all of them share one time axis, so each bar sits at its place in time and the same date lines up from timeline to timeline. Tasks that start before or finish after their timeline's range end in an arrow on the bar, or a pointed marker for a callout. "Today" is a global marker in blue; "Budget review" is a marker on Delivery and Operations only, labelled below. Drag a marker's label to move it, or to the other end of its line.

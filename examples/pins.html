Pins, text templates and callout layers

Click a task bar, milestone, callout or marker label, then use the checkbox to pin or unpin it (setEntryDraggable, setMarkerDraggable). A pinned item shows 📌, drawn by the _text templates, and can't be dragged. Some items are pinned in the data with draggable: false. Hover anything for a tooltip from tooltip_text and tooltip_class. The labels come from milestone_text, callout_text and marker_text. The purple band on the bar's edge is a callout layer, placed with getCalloutPosition.

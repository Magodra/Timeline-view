Timeline view examples

  • Basic: one timeline with a task and a milestone callout.
  • Features: several timelines, month scale, milestone symbols, labels at different heights, a second view with absolute timelines and tasks that continue past their range, and selection with external buttons to move the selected timeline.
  • Drag in from a list: dragging tasks and milestones from a list outside the view onto it, with targetAt, highlightTarget and addEntry.
  • SVG export: a download button outside the component that saves the view as SVG.
  • REST backend: loading and saving callout moves through the REST API (mock server).

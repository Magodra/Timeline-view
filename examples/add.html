Drag in from a list

Drag a task or milestone from the list onto a bar line, the half line above or below the bar's lines (opens a new line), or a callout area above or below a bar. The target under the pointer is highlighted. A task can go on several timelines, but only once on each. Press Escape to cancel. To take a task off a timeline, click it in the view and press "Remove from timeline" (removeEntry); the task stays in the data.
